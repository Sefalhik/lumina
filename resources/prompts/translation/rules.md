Rules for every text:

- Translate every word. A French word left in the {{language}} text is always an error, even in a
  short tagline, even next to a term that stays as it is.
- These terms are invariable — write them exactly as they are, and translate the words around them:
  {{glossary}}.
- The names of people, of software, of libraries and of standards stay as they are. Nothing else
  does: a word that looks technical is translated like any other unless it is one of the terms
  above.
- Keep line breaks, and the typographic conventions of {{language}} for quotation marks,
  apostrophes and spaces.

You receive a JSON object of French texts. Answer with a JSON object that has exactly the same keys
and the {{language}} translations as values — the object alone, without any other text.
